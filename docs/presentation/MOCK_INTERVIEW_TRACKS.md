# Mock Interview Tracks

Version 1.14A adds preset mock interview tracks.

## Tracks

### Quant Researcher
Balanced research-style interview with probability, statistics, derivatives, stochastic calculus, time series, ML, and coding.

### Quant Developer
Coding-heavy track for C++, Python, algorithms, numerical methods, market data, and systems topics.

### Derivatives Pricing
Derivatives, Greeks, stochastic calculus, numerical methods, and probability.

### Probability & Brainteasers
Probability foundations, expected value puzzles, and classic brainteasers.

### Statistics & Machine Learning
Statistics, time series, machine learning, validation, and Python.

## How to use

1. Open the `Mock Interview` tab.
2. Choose a track.
3. Select difficulty and status.
4. Choose the number of questions.
5. Start the mock interview.
6. Reveal answers after thinking.
7. Self-assess each question.
8. Review the final summary and weak topics.
